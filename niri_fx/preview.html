NiriFX Studio

LOCAL STUDIO

Shape how windows open and close. Explore fragments, slices and elastic motion, then save your own style.

Design preview only. Niri needs a compositor extension for these movements. Saving and exporting affect open/close and enabled resize animations. The experimental native movement demo runs separately.

Saving adds a style to Settings → Windows → Movement → Style. Your current animation stays active until you select another style there.
